##  omnipotent, unscrupulous, renaissance, genesis, warrant

An unscrupulous thief went into an art gallery which exhibited works from the cultural renaissance end of medieval era. One of them depicted an omnipotent warrior fighting beasts, drawn by a famous artist. The genesis of an idea came up in the thief's mind: "Unless there was another thief steals it first, I would put it in my bag". A few hours later, the police obtained a warrant to put the thief in jail, otherwise the police station of this district would be punished.
