##  jargon, prudent, inviolable, commodious, proximity

My neighbor just came back from another city. He is a computer engineer who majored in computer science in university. And he always uses jargon that I do not understand. His house is commodious and decorates in a luxurious manner. Because of the proximity of our houses, we were close until he got a job in another city. We had made a vow when we were kids, which we would be the best friends for life, and that vow is inviolable. We prudently keep it.
