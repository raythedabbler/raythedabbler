##  resolute, supposition. arbitrary, monotonuous, legacy

My grandmother passed away last month. She wrote a will which was resolute in dealing with her legacy. "If there had been someone accompany me when i was died, all my legacy would belong to him or her." This was her supposition. Not only was she a monotonous woman, but she also told us the same stories hundreds of times. Furthermore, she liked to make some arbitrary decitions, as if refusing go to the hospital because it tested her too much——she felt those tests were not necessary.
