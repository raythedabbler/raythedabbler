##    manifold, pliant, retort, obstinate, lacerate

My neighbors are a couple. The personalities of the man and woman are polar opposites. The woman always makes a clever retort when people asked her questions. However, the man is obstinate. Once he decides his opinion or his course of action, he will never change. One day, the woman came home late, because of manifold tasks. The man lacerated his forearm while he was fixing a sharp pipe. He would not go to the hospital, because he did not trust doctors at all. When the woman came back, the man was bending a pliant wire to stop the bleeding. The woman screamed and yelled at him :"what a fool! If only you would listen to me, you would not get hurt!"
